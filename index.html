Quant

UWB radar based human vital signal and range detection beyond obstacles

IEEE CONECCT 2024, published on IEEE Xplore

Coauthored a peer reviewed paper on extracting breathing and heartbeat signals from through wall radar data. Proposed a clutter reduction pipeline using SVD, exponential weight subtraction and moving average filtering that outperformed Fourier based baselines, then reconstructed vital signs with Empirical Mode Decomposition.

Why it matters here: the same toolkit of decomposing and denoising signals carries straight over to financial time series.

Read the paper on IEEE Xplore

Moving average crossover backtester in C++

Personal project, 2024

A command line backtester that loads historical OHLC data from CSV, simulates a moving average crossover strategy, tracks positions and realised P&L, and reports trades, win rate and cumulative return. Rolling windows are computed with std::deque.

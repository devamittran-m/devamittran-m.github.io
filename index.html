About

I'm a data scientist at Turing, where I work with Google as the client on the backend of training the Gemini models. Alongside that, I'm at IIT Kanpur, where my focus has shifted toward financial signal processing: estimation, filtering and forecasting on market data.

The question I keep coming back to is how large pretrained models — trained on text or on huge collections of time series — fit into a field built on classical statistical methods. My literature survey is where I'm working that out.
